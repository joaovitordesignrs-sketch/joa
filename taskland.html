01 — Origin

Breaking out of procrastination to build something with soul

After a period of creative stagnation, I went back to studying, experimenting, and really putting in the work on something entirely my own — like I hadn't done in a long time.

The goal was clear from day one: go against the tide of generic, soulless apps mass-produced with AI. Bring personality back to the digital landscape.

04 — The Hard Lesson

AI can make things look good. Testing reveals the truth.

The first version looked polished and even functional. But rigorous testing revealed critical weaknesses under real-world conditions.

I rebuilt Taskland from scratch — this time with a clear architectural foundation, proper data architecture, and a comprehensive plan in hand. No shortcuts.

Architecture First

Clear separation of concerns, proper data modeling, and a plan before writing a single line of code.

Rive Animations

Stress Testing

Every feature tested under real conditions. The rebuild yielded tangible improvements in reliability.

The lesson that forced a complete rebuild

05 — The Product

Combining retro games with real productivity

  • Campaign mode — tasks become missions against monsters. Completing tasks deals damage. There's always a next fight.
  • 3-click task creation — type name, pick difficulty, Enter. Zero friction.
  • Habits as power multipliers — real-life consistency translates directly into in-game power.
  • Achievement system — Bronze through Legendary tiers with permanent damage bonuses.
  • Cross-device cloud sync — Supabase real-time sync across desktop and mobile.
